Sip and Paint Sessions

Unleash your inner artist in a relaxed, inspiring atmosphere! Our Sip & Paint sessions are perfect for beginners and hobbyists alike. Each session is guided by a local artist and includes all the materials you need—canvas, paints, brushes, and an apron. Plus, enjoy a complimentary drink of your choice (coffee, tea, or juice) while you create your masterpiece.

Pricing:
Regular Session: ₱799 per person (2 hours)
Themed Event Nights: ₱899 per person (includes a pastry + drink)

Schedule:
Every Friday & Saturday, 6PM – 8PM
Private bookings available for groups of 6+
