~About Us~


Welcome to Atelier de Café—where creativity meets aroma. A cozy haven for dreamers, doers, and everyday artists, we offer a space to sip your favorite brew, create your next masterpiece, or simply pause and relax.

Our menu features artisanal coffee, handcrafted pastries, and creative fuel to nourish both body and soul. Whether you're enjoying Sip & Paint nights or working in our Creative Co-Working Lounge, we invite you to explore, express, and connect.

Atelier de Café is more than a café; it’s a community, a shared space for inspiration and conversation. Come for the coffee, stay for the creativity.
